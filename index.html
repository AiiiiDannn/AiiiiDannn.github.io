About Me

Aiden is fascinated by how people and intelligent systems can understand and help each other, not just through technology, but through real experiences. Double majoring in Cognitive Science and Informatics at UC Irvine, with a minor in Computer Science, my journey has always been about exploring how human thinking, decision-making, and interaction can be turned into real, usable technology.

I'm especially interested in designing and implementing systems, whether that means building smart AI, crafting intuitive user interfaces, or organizing the logic behind the scenes. What excites me most is translating complex ideas into tools that people actually want to use, and making technology feel approachable and natural. Rather than just chasing performance, I care most about whether a system is understandable and genuinely helpful.

When I'm not at my desk, you'll probably find me experimenting in the kitchen, going for a workout, or getting clutched on (and dying) in Counter Strike. I'm always open to new challenges, collaborations, or just good conversations about how we can make things a little more human.
